Original Mission Plan

The GWU CubeSat origionally started in the late 2010's long prior to my arrival at GWU. The original mission goal was to test experimental solid fuel thrusters, create a digipeater relay, and monitor wetlands in Costa Rica. This project initially was a large team working in collaboration with the Costa Rica Institute of Technology (TEC) and the United States Naval Academy which aimed to support the ADCS and telemetry problems respectively. When Covid game around the majority of the old guard graduated and knowledge failed to be passed down as a result of online school. The project remained dormant until myself and some others decided to revive it. Now with a smaller team (3 of us) we aim to consolodate the resources that we do have and focus on the goals that are feasible to produce a working 3U cube satellite now slated for CRS 37 of August '27.

A Revival

The project, with a smaller team, has narrowed its focus and aims to complete the following items:

  • Test Cathode Arc - Solid Fuel Thrusters
  • Design & Manufacture a Digipeater (A Radio Tower for Space)
  • Demonstrate full data control - Start to Finish
