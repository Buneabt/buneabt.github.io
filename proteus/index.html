Proteus

Working with GW-Ground and GW-Sat I've taken a liking to RF systems and the "cool" factor of being able to communicate with other people, across vast distances, through means we can't see with the naked eye. In the past few years I've started researching more into this field taking ECE classes (Signals & Transforms and Applied Electromagnetics) while also getting my amateur radio license with the FCC (KQ4NPQ). Working on GW-Sat I realized having a proper amount of margin in your link budget is what a good comm system should strive for but most CubeSats utilize a fixed radiation pattern system. Proteus will give teams a single antenna that can accomplish omni-directional and directional radiation patterns. Current idea it to utilize a QHA with a four feed unit for beam forming capabilities. This page will also go through my journey of learning Simcenter FEKO, an important tool that will help validate the antenna through-out its entire design process.

Learning FEKO

Installing FEKO I've figured the best thing to do is to go through the example guide constructing and simulating different antennas. To the right you can see the first example from the guide, a simple Dipole antenna w/ its radiation pattern. The next thing I wanted to create is a radiation pattern for the Arrow II 440-7, a 7 element yagi antenna for the 70 cm band. Notoriously they do not publish Radiation pattern figures but we do have the build specs! Therefore we can create the antenna used in the first few versions of the ground station. (WIP)
