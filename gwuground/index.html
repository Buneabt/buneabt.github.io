GW-Ground

Previous mission parameters relied on the United States Naval Academy (USNA) to provide telemetry capabilites but with the new team that has shifted to a GWU based ground station designed, built, and operated by students. In re-establishing connections with mentors at the Naval Academy I was invited out for a site visit to assemble their next generation of antennas for the purpose of cubesat communication (we weren't the only school). In this experience I was greatful of the knowledge given to me by my mentors and have applied their ideas to GW-Ground.

The Design

Given GW-Ground is an in-house solution to telemetry with a short timespan several requirements needed to be met in the elicitation stage during the summer 26'. The two major functional requirements are: 1) The System shall be mounted on a semi-permantent installation 2) The Software Defined Radio (SDR) shall produce modulated RF signals. The first requirement takes into account the lengthy approval processing for roof mounting while the second is simply as a result of our onboard transceiver using GFSK modulation. Given this second requirement the SDR of choice for this project is the LimeSDR Mini which is ample in it's capabilities we priced against its competitors.
