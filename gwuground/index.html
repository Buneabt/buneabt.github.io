GW-Ground

Previous mission parameters relied on the United States Naval Academy (USNA) to provide telemetry capabilites but with the new team that has shifted to a GWU based ground station designed, built, and operated by students. In re-establishing connections with mentors at the Naval Academy I was invited out for a site visit to assemble their next generation of antennas for the purpose of cubesat communication (we weren't the only school). In this experience I was greatful of the knowledge given to me by my mentors and have applied their ideas to GW-Ground.

A Revival

The project, with a smaller team, has narrowed its focus and aims to complete the following items:

  • Test Cathode Arc - Solid Fuel Thrusters
  • Design & Manufacture a Digipeater (A Radio Tower for Space)
  • Demonstrate full data control - Start to Finish
